Good to know

Do people need an app?

No. Your card opens your profile in their phone's browser.

Which phones work?

Most modern iPhones and Android phones can read NFC cards.

Do I have to show everything?

Not at all. Every line has its own switch, so you only show what you choose.

How do I get a card?

Message us on Facebook and we'll sort out your Lazy card. Once you have it, set up your page right here.

How do I set up my page?

Open Set up my page, pick a design, add your details, and you get a personal link. Write that link onto your Lazy card with any free NFC app and you are done.

Can I change my details later?

Yes. Open the setup, paste your link to load your card, change what you like, and write the new link to your card.

Want something custom?

Choose Customize LazyCard and message us on Facebook. We design your card and your website around you.
